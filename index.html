About the model

Positions are calculated with NASA JPL’s long-range Keplerian elements, including corrections for the outer planets. JPL’s fitted elements cover 3000 BCE–3000 CE. After 3000 CE, the orbital paths are illustrative extrapolations. Earth uses the Earth–Moon barycenter. This is not a precision ephemeris or eclipse predictor.

All dates use the proleptic Gregorian calendar, including dates before its introduction. UTC dates approximate the ephemeris timescale. Sizes are enlarged. Compact mode also compresses orbit distances; Proportional mode preserves their ratios. Surface rotation and axial orientations are illustrative. Today’s Earth texture is reused for historical dates. The Moon and optional Pluto follow illustrative orbits, not the eight-planet JPL solution.
